# Maintenance
<!-- order: 50 -->

Under **Robot → Maintenance**: timers counted from what the robot actually did, not from the calendar alone.

- Units: blade-motor hours, km driven, mows, runs, charge cycles, days, or once per head change.
- Items start at Yarbo's own advice where Yarbo gives a number (blades every 3 months, tracks greased every two
  weeks, battery at 500 cycles), with a link to Yarbo's page. Where Yarbo only says "regularly", set your own.
- **Mark done** when you do the job; the count starts again.
- An item at 85 % is "due soon"; at 100 % it is overdue and an alert is sent (checked daily).
- Add your own items at the foot of the page.
