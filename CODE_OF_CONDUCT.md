# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background or experience. Be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism
about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information
(including where they live, which this project's data can reveal) are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs that break this, and may block people who keep doing it.
To report something privately, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

This follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
